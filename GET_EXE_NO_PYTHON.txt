GET A READY-TO-RUN KBM2Pad.exe WITHOUT INSTALLING PYTHON
(uses GitHub's free servers to build it for you)

1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (name it kbm2pad, Public or Private).
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in EVERYTHING from this folder,
   including the hidden .github folder (easiest: unzip, then drag the whole contents in).
   Commit the upload.
3. Open the Actions tab -> "Build KBM2Pad exe" -> Run workflow. Wait ~2 minutes.
4. Open the finished run, scroll to Artifacts, download KBM2Pad-exe.zip, unzip -> KBM2Pad.exe.

THEN, on your PC (one time):
- Install the ViGEmBus driver: github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus/releases (run the setup, reboot).
- Double-click KBM2Pad.exe. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app is unsigned:
  click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
